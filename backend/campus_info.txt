=== OFFICIAL CAMPUS INFORMATION KNOWLEDGE BASE ===
1. University Library:
   - Location: Center Quad, Building 4
   - Regular Hours: Monday-Thursday 7:00 AM - Midnight, Friday 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM, Saturday-Sunday 10:00 AM - 10:00 PM.
   - Special feature: 24/7 study lab on the 1st floor during finals week.

2. Student Union & Dining:
   - Location: North Campus near the main fountain
   - Dining Options: 'Campus Grill' (burgers/fries), 'Taco Terra' (Mexican), 'Green Leaf' (salads/wraps).
   - Hours: 7:30 AM - 9:00 PM daily.

3. Campus Recreation Center (The Rec):
   - Location: South Campus, next to the stadium
   - Amenities: Olympic pool, rock climbing wall, full gym, indoor basketball courts.
   - Hours: 6:00 AM - 11:00 PM on weekdays, 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM on weekends.

4. Parking & Transportation:
   - Student parking permits required in Lots A, B, and C.
   - Campus shuttle runs every 15 minutes between North Quad and South Campus from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM.
